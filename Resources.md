Below are some helpful initial resources to read after the first session:

* **[Craig Reynolds' Boids Algorithm](https://www.red3d.com/cwr/boids/)** – An introduction/explanation of Craig Reynold's flocking and swarm model of boids.
* **[Boids Pseudocode & Mathematical Breakdown](https://www.vergenet.net/~conrad/boids/pseudocode.html)** – A mathematical breakdown of the boids algorithm.
* **[H Bridge Motor Speed Controller Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iYafyPZ15g8)** How H-bridge motor drivers work.
* **[Motor speed controller tutorial - PWM  ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UPTU6nYSaMo)** How Pulse-Width Modulation (PWM) is used in directional motor control. 
* **[ESP32 & HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N9vl7ZlQAdI)** – A DIY Servo project using an ESP32 MCU and our HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors.
